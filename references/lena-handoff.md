# Worked example: the Lena Komarova episode (published 1 October 2026)

Watch it: https://youtu.be/81fguxFhLPA. Yousif approved v6 and published it on AI Cross Fields.

## How it got there

- Yousif rejected the first versions for generic graphics and default fonts, and asked for Diary of a CEO informed craft.
- He approved the v3 music, buildup and closing structure, then asked for the real AI Cross Fields logo, colours and matching typography, with fully readable words.
- v6 then: identified the host once, gave Lena the same black name panel, and replaced seven text-only explanations with original speech-timed drawings.

## What the final v6 contains

- 36-second teaser built from real lines, with the opening zooms, emphasis words and Scott Buckley "Signal to Noise" music.
- One-time name cards for Yussef Idris and Lena Komarova: black panel, white slanted Anton, amber rule.
- Seven drawn explanations (native HyperFrames SVG/GSAP), alternating full-frame drawings and drawing-beside-portrait layouts; one CC0 city photo, labelled as illustrative.
- A labelled detector illustration (not a real detector result) and a "YOUR EDGE / YOUR TECHNOLOGY / YOUR STORY" motion graphic.
- Calm, mostly unscored interview; quiet no-melody music under the closing; real logo end card with WHERE AI MEETS EVERY INDUSTRY.
- 1080p 24 fps, 27:03, −16.5 LUFS, true peak −1.9 dBTP.

## Lessons that carry over

- The Riverside combined export had six black or silent gaps. They were repaired with real same-episode excerpts, and the separate tracks were the clean source. Download separate tracks first.
- A concat remux shifted the picture by 21 ms through AAC priming. Check sync at the start, middle and end of the delivered file.
- Captions were an automatic draft and were never proofread or uploaded. Say so if that is still true.
- Report honestly what you checked by watching and listening, and what you only measured.
