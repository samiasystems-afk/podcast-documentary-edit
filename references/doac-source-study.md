## First-person process

[Anthony Smith interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4xBkm13C3iU): **20:58–21:46** starts from the episode's story; **31:37–32:39** text experimentation and music supporting emotion; **33:05–34:52** isolate the tested variable; **43:29–44:08** alternative narrative arcs; **45:04–45:56 and 48:15–49:10** select real material, write the narrative, assemble dialogue, then music/B-roll/type/ratios; **49:17–49:52** separate music, accents and visual review passes. Reported experiments do not prove future results for our show.

[Direct editor Q&A, 7 October 2022](https://becauseofmarketing.com/anthony-smith-the-diary-of-a-ceo/), questions 3–4: full-interview selection and a compact narrative precede finishing. Historical method evidence, not current staffing or a proprietary recipe.
