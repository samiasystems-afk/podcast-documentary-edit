# Fast pace: what Yousif approved on 23 October 2026

Yousif's Arabic edit "7 habits I run my company with" (a replica of Nick Saraev's "How to Use AI Like a Top 0.1% CEO") was approved with "that looks much better". He wants the same pace, zooms, transitions and B-roll on AI Cross Fields, **branded to the podcast** (ink #0C0C0C, white #FAFAFA, amber #F09701, Anton headings, the real logo). This overrides the calmer "let the interview settle" pace used for Lena. Keep Lena's brand, name cards, drawings and music.

The approved build was Remotion at 1920×1080, 30 fps. The numbers below are what shipped; adapt frame counts if the source is 24 fps.

## 1. Cut tight

- Remove retakes and false starts. When a line is said twice, keep the better take.
- Put every cut inside a measured silence, never mid-word.
- Shorten pauses longer than 0.45 s to 0.22 s. Never tighten a pause if it would leave a piece shorter than 0.5 s.
- Keep a pause only when it carries meaning (a laugh, a real moment of thought).

## 2. Punch-in on every jump cut

- Alternate the framing between 1.00 and 1.12 scale on consecutive pieces from the same source, centred on the measured face (face centre x, about 0.55 of face height down).
- Reset to 1.00 when the source or camera changes.
- Never zoom so far that the face, a name card or the subscribe animation collides.

## 3. The face hook stays clean

- The first ~6–7 seconds are the speaker's face, with no card, B-roll or text over it.
- B-roll begins after the hook lands.

## 4. B-roll cards for most of the runtime

- Target about 80% of the runtime on cards (the approved edit: 55 cards, 583 s of 739 s, 79%).
- Card types used: list, checklist, tiles, flow, chat mock-up, big statement, real page, document, quotes, bars, call to action.
- Keep the speaker visible as a small face tile beside the card: same footage, same frame, cropped around the face.
- **Anchor each card to the spoken phrase.** A card starts 0.3 s before its first item or title, never earlier. No empty card on screen.
- **Each item appears on the word that names it.** Never reveal a list before it is said.
- Use a card to cover any moment where the speaker looks away from the camera for more than about 3 seconds. The approved edit checked eye direction for this.
- Use real pages (the guest's site, a real product listing) where the claim is about something that exists. Label mock-ups as illustrations. Never invent numbers or results.
- Use the skill's drawings for the bigger explanations.

## 5. Transitions

- Face → card: the card slides in from the right over 9 frames, ease-out, no bounce.
- Card → face: it slides out to the left over 9 frames.
- Card → card: no slide; the paper stays still and the content changes.
- Sound: a very soft whoosh under each slide (0.32 s band-passed pink noise, peak about −26 dBFS), felt more than heard.

## 6. Chapter stingers

- At each new chapter, a short full-frame ink panel with the chapter number and title.
- A soft chime under it (the approved bell peaked at −12 dBFS, ~1.3 s decay).
- Chapters in the description must match these.

## 7. Term chips

- When a jargon word comes up for the first time (for example ACoS, PPC, LLM), show a small chip in the lower third: the term plus its plain-English meaning. Once per term.
- The approved edit had 19 chips. Stack a second chip above the first if two terms land close together.

## 8. Subscribe and comments

- A short subscribe animation in the first minute: low right, clear of the face, slides in, a cursor clicks it, it flips to "Subscribed", the bell rings, it slides out (~3.3 s) with a soft click sound.
- If the guest or host asks viewers a question, place it near the first useful answer, not only at the end.

## 9. Sound

- Speech first: −14 LUFS for YouTube speech in the approved edit (−16 LUFS is fine for the podcast), true peak −1.5 dBTP, gentle compression, high-pass at 70 Hz.
- Effects measured to their peak target, then a limiter.

## 10. Check before calling it done

- Watch the frames: each card's start, middle and end, every chip, every stinger, the subscribe animation and the end card.
- Confirm no card sits in the face hook, every card shows content when it appears, and nothing covers the face.
- Check the delivered file: frame count, duration, audio length, loudness, no black or frozen spans.
- A passing check is not the same as looking. Open frames and watch the preview before sending it to Yousif.
