# Questions and packaging

Use for requested preparation or interpreting interview structure in an edit. These are our adaptable prompts, not DOAC quotes. See [source study](doac-source-study.md).

Write one viewer promise. Research the guest's real decisions, examples, disagreements and limits. Save **purpose | verified premise/source | main question | follow-up | evidence/prop | viewer payoff**. Listen to the answer instead of forcing a checklist.

Useful progression: why this problem matters now → specific incident/decision → mechanism/consequence → fair challenge/limits → practical demonstration → meaningful reflection/next problem. Origin belongs early when it explains behaviour; it is not a compulsory biography before value.

| Need | Original question adaptation |
|---|---|
| Scene | Take me to the first time this happened. What did you send, and what came back? |
| Meaning | You called it disrespectful. What makes it different from using AI to prepare a report? |
| Mechanism | Walk me through the steps before and after you introduced it. |
| Evidence | Is that one case or a pattern? What can you show us? |
| Consequence | What did the mistake cost in time, trust or a lost opportunity? |
| Fair challenge | What would the person on the other side say happened? |
| Limits | When would you advise a team against doing this? |
| Demonstration | Show us the version you would actually send. What did you change? |
| Next action | If a small team tries one thing tomorrow, where should it start? |

Ask one clear question at a time; follow unexpected specifics. Clarify numbers/jargon and separate anecdotes from general claims. Reflect the guest's words without supplying emotions. Preserve useful thinking space. A real document, comparison, photograph or screen demo can make a natural scene; confirm provenance and permission. Sensitive life history is not mandatory for a strong business episode. Never fabricate new questions in existing recorded footage.

When packaging is requested, develop distinct title/thumbnail mechanisms around real value: surprising failure, practical decision, visible transformation. Compare title/image together at mobile size. Connect **package → opening proof → full answer → relevant next step**. Avoid unsupported numbers, endorsement or borrowed celebrity authority.

Our sampled DOAC episodes include substantial post-teaser subscription appeals; that does not establish the right length/timing for AI Cross Fields. Give a short relevant reason after value and return to the interview. Do not copy private non-subscriber percentages or signature phrases. Prepare description/chapters/captions/credits within task scope; publication remains separately authorised.

Use Yousif's video brief fields where he supplies them inside the existing episode brief. Adapt audience fields to English AI Cross Fields; never blend with Arabic. Measure CTR/retention/subscriber outcomes after publication. Do not promise them from style alone.
